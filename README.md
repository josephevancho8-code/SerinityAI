🚀 Serinity AI v0.1.0-Alpha (Pre-Release / Architecture Preview)
A Sovereign, Local-First Operational Co-Pilot & Ecosystem
Welcome to the internal pre-release and architectural preview of Serinity—a bespoke, privacy-first AI intelligence engineered to run entirely on your local hardware with zero mandatory cloud dependencies, zero subscriptions, and complete user control.
While many tools exist to run large language models locally, Serinity goes further. She is not just a chat interface; she is designed as a complete local-first operating intelligence—bridging full-stack software development, field service business management (Dovely Solutions), and physical technical craftsmanship within an absolute local-first state.
⚡ Current Development Status (v0.1.0-Alpha)
Serinity is currently in active, hands-on alpha testing and development:
Terminal Backend Operational: The core local Python/FastAPI engine paired with Ollama (qwen2.5:3b) is fully functional in the terminal, delivering zero-latency offline inference.
Persistent SQLite MemoryStore (sqflite): Structured local database architecture is actively storing data and context across sessions.
Active Work (Current Sprint): Ironing out network connectivity layers and online file-searching capabilities to seamlessly bridge local privacy with necessary web retrieval.
Platform Target: Optimized primarily for local Linux environments right now, with Windows compatibility targeted for subsequent development milestones.

🔒 The Local-First Guarantee: Your Data, Your Rules
100% Offline Capable: Powered by local LLM inference running offline on your own hardware. Your queries and files stay local unless you explicitly authorize an encrypted tunnel (such as Google Drive or Dropbox).
Persistent Local State: All structured data and records remain under your direct control via local SQLite.
Absolute Ownership: You hold the keys. No corporate telemetry, no sudden API deprecations, and no hidden tracking.
